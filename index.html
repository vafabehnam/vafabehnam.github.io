Vafa Behnam

Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in economics at UC San Diego, and I will be on the 2026–2027 job market.

I study trade and industrial policies in a variety of contemporary and historical settings, using novel cross-sectional designs that exploit differential across-region, within-sector exposure to nationwide policy changes. I quantify several general equilibrium and spillover channels — through structural models or direct estimation — to speak to some of the aggregate implications of these policies.

Before graduate school, I received a double BA in Mathematics-Economics and Computer Science from Brown University. I then worked as a full-time research assistant at the National Bureau of Economic Research.
